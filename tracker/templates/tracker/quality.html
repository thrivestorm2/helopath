Known blind spots

  • Aircraft whose hospital landings happen below receiver coverage are never discovered.
  • A hospital missing from the site table hides every aircraft that uses only that hospital.
  • False positives: charter, maintenance, organ or blood transport, and law-enforcement helicopters that use a hospital pad repeatedly. The review queue exists for these.
  • Military and non-US-registered helicopters are outside the candidate pool (FAA rotorcraft registry).
  • Landings are inferred from ADS-B positions. "Mission" labels are rule-based guesses; none asserts that a patient was carried.
